# References and data use

Sources were checked on **2 November 2026**. Web maps and access conditions can change. The repository has no affiliation with or endorsement from the organizations below.

## Primary mapping resources

- [Allen Coral Atlas](https://allencoralatlas.org/) and [science and methods](https://allencoralatlas.org/methods/): habitat maps and monitoring tools; the website now describes version 2 maps.
- [Coral Reef Alliance: 2021 Atlas launch](https://coral.org/blog/the-allen-coral-atlas-introduces-the-first-comprehensive-map-of-shallow-water-coral-reefs/): historical context for completion of the first global habitat map.
- [KSLOF: Mapping the World's Coral Reefs](https://www.livingoceansfoundation.org/mapping-the-worlds-coral-reefs/): expedition mapping, NASA collaboration, and data-request instructions.
- [KSLOF World Reef Map](https://maps.lof.org/): an interactive viewing portal.
- [NASA: Landsat 7 worldwide inventory of coral reefs completed](https://landsat.gsfc.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2012/07/coral-l7.pdf): historical account of Millennium mapping.
- [IMaRS Millennium Corals](https://imars.usf.edu/millennium-corals/): original project resource. This page timed out during review; NASA and the IMaRS homepage provide supporting historical context.
- [UNEP-WCMC global reef dataset record](https://data-gis.unep-wcmc.org/portal/home/item.html?id=0613604367334836863f5c0c10e452bf): inspect the live metadata, citation, and access terms before downloading. The interactive record did not expose readable metadata to the review tool.
- [Ocean+ Habitats dataset access guidance](https://habitats.oceanplus.org/): UNEP-WCMC directs former Ocean+ Data Viewer users to its GIS Portal.
- [WHO: One Health](https://www.who.int/health-topics/one-health): background on the cross-sector approach.

## One Health readings and evidence

- Sweet, M., Burian, A., and Bulling, M. (2021). **Corals as canaries in the coalmine: Towards the incorporation of marine ecosystems into the 'One Health' concept.** *Journal of Invertebrate Pathology*, 186, 107538. [DOI: 10.1016/j.jip.2021.107538](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jip.2021.107538) · [PubMed, PMID 33545133](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33545133/). The indexed abstract supports the framework discussion; the full text was not reviewed.
- Coral Reef Alliance (13 January 2025). [The Surprising Connections Between Coral Reefs and Human Health](https://coral.org/blog/the-surprising-connections-between-coral-reefs-and-human-health/). A public-facing introduction used for critical discussion, not a clinical evidence review.
- NOAA. [Coral reef ecosystems](https://www.noaa.gov/education/resource-collections/marine-life/coral-reef-ecosystems). Background on ecosystem services and cultural connections.
- McGowan, H. A., MacKellar, M. C., and Gray, M. A. (2016). **Direct measurements of air-sea CO₂ exchange over a coral reef.** *Geophysical Research Letters*, 43, 4602–4608. [DOI: 10.1002/2016GL068772](https://agupubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/2016GL068772). A direct-measurement case study showing spatial variation in carbon exchange.

## Research discovery and external tools

- **luismmontilla and contributors.** [Awesome coral research](https://github.com/luismmontilla/awesome-coral-research). Discovery source for the selected research-tool links. Its [license file](https://github.com/luismmontilla/awesome-coral-research/blob/master/LICENCE.md) declares [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Our guide selects links, paraphrases descriptions, and adds original learning activities; no directory image or full list is copied.
- **NOAA Coral Reef Watch.** [Satellite monitoring and products portal](https://coralreefwatch.noaa.gov/satellite/index.php). Source for the heat-stress and bleaching-alert product description.
- **MERMAID team / Wildlife Conservation Society.** [MERMAID](https://datamermaid.org/). Source for survey collection, exploration, and management capabilities.
- **CoralNet team.** [CoralNet](https://coralnet.ucsd.edu/). Source for image organization, annotation, and annotation statistics.
- **OBIS contributors.** [Ocean Biodiversity Information System](https://obis.org/). Source for marine biodiversity data discovery.

These services are linked for further learning and are not bundled into JupyterLite. Data citations and access conditions should be obtained from the relevant provider when using an actual dataset.
