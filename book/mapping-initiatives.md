# Global reef mapping initiatives

Reef maps answer different questions. Before combining products, check the date of imagery, habitat classes, spatial resolution, depth and geographic coverage, validation methods, and data-use terms.

| Initiative | Main contribution | Good starting question | Key limitation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Allen Coral Atlas | Global shallow tropical reef habitat mapping and monitoring tools | Where are mapped reef zones and potential bleaching or turbidity signals? | Remote observations and mapped classes need local interpretation |
| KSLOF Global Reef Expedition | Detailed habitat and bathymetry maps supported by field surveys | What can field validation add to habitat mapping? | Expedition sites do not constitute continuous global coverage |
| Millennium Coral Reef Mapping Project | A foundational Landsat-based global reef inventory | Where did historical mapping establish a consistent baseline? | Historical imagery and coarser pixels limit contemporary local interpretation |

## Allen Coral Atlas

The Atlas provides benthic and geomorphic reef maps alongside bleaching and turbidity monitoring. Its first global habitat map was completed in September 2021; its current website describes version 2 habitat maps. Its methods distinguish geomorphic zonation mapped to approximately 15 m and benthic composition to approximately 10 m; actual visibility depends on water conditions. [Allen Coral Atlas](https://allencoralatlas.org/) · [Science and methods](https://allencoralatlas.org/methods/) · [2021 launch account](https://coral.org/blog/the-allen-coral-atlas-introduces-the-first-comprehensive-map-of-shallow-water-coral-reefs/)

**Try it:** open the Atlas, locate a reef you know, and record the legend, product version, imagery dates, and uncertainty information. A coral/algae habitat label alone cannot establish live coral cover, species diversity, or health outcomes.

## Khaled bin Sultan Living Oceans Foundation

The Global Reef Expedition combined satellite imagery with field observations to map more than 1,000 reefs across the Atlantic, Pacific, Indian Oceans, and Red Sea. KSLOF reports mapping about 65,000 km² of shallow reef habitat. Its 2021 NASA partnership makes expedition maps available to train NeMO-Net; this is distinct from assuming that a completed new global product is already downloadable. [Project and NASA collaboration](https://www.livingoceansfoundation.org/mapping-the-worlds-coral-reefs/) · [World Reef Map](https://maps.lof.org/)

**Try it:** compare a mapped expedition site with an Atlas location. Ask whether the products use the same habitat classes and observation dates. KSLOF provides a data request process; viewing a map does not establish redistribution permission.

## Millennium Coral Reef Mapping Project

The NASA-supported project at IMaRS-USF, with IRD collaboration, used Landsat 7 imagery to create a consistent global shallow reef inventory. NASA's historical summary describes the completed inventory; UNEP-WCMC distributes a global coral reef compilation that incorporates Millennium mapping with other sources. [NASA historical summary](https://landsat.gsfc.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2012/07/coral-l7.pdf) · [IMaRS project](https://imars.usf.edu/millennium-corals/) · [UNEP-WCMC dataset record](https://data-gis.unep-wcmc.org/portal/home/item.html?id=0613604367334836863f5c0c10e452bf)

**Try it:** distinguish original survey dates from a dataset's publication or update date. A compilation's release date does not mean every reef boundary was observed that year.

## Compare responsibly

Record the product version, imagery period, coordinate reference system, units, class definitions, and license before analysis. Reproject data when necessary, align spatial support, and document why comparisons are meaningful. Missing observations are not evidence of absent reefs.
